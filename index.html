What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?

(Purple Star Astrology)

On this page

  • Overview
  • Common Questions
  • Get your chart

Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數), often called Purple Star Astrology in English, is the most granular system in Chinese metaphysics. Developed during the Song Dynasty for imperial court use, it maps your birth moment onto a 12-palace board populated by over 100 stars — revealing patterns in career, wealth, relationships, health, and timing that simpler systems cannot reach.

Unlike Western astrology's sun-sign generalizations, a Zi Wei chart is built from your exact birth date, birth hour (a two-hour time branch), and ideally your birth location for true solar time. Each of the twelve palaces governs a life domain: Life (self), Wealth, Career, Spouse, Health, Travel, and more. The interaction of major stars like Zi Wei (Purple Emperor), Wu Qu (Commander), and Tai Yin (Lunar Nobility) creates a unique blueprint no two people share — and works for anyone worldwide, regardless of birthplace.

Common Questions

Is Zi Wei Dou Shu more accurate than BaZi?

They answer different questions. BaZi distills your elemental balance into eight characters; Zi Wei maps every life domain palace-by-palace. Serious practitioners use both — BaZi for energy diagnosis, Zi Wei for situational timing and domain-specific guidance.

Do I need my exact birth hour?

Yes. The birth hour determines palace placement and the Life Palace position. Without it, the chart shifts entirely. Our calculator accepts standard two-hour branches (Zi, Chou, Yin…), wherever you were born in the world.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Zi Wei Dou Shu more accurate than BaZi?

BaZi and Zi Wei Dou Shu serve different purposes. BaZi reveals your elemental composition and 10-year luck cycles in eight characters. Zi Wei maps twelve life domains with star-level detail — career, wealth, relationships, health, and more — each receiving a unique constellation. Many serious practitioners use both together for the most complete picture of timing and character.

Why do I need my exact birth hour for a Zi Wei chart?

The birth hour determines which of the twelve palaces becomes your Life Palace — the anchor for the entire chart. A difference of one two-hour branch shifts every star placement and changes the reading entirely. Our calculator uses the traditional Chinese two-hour branch system (Zi, Chou, Yin…), which applies regardless of where in the world you were born.
