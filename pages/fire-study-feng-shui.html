🔥 Fire Element Study Room — Feng Shui Guide for Fire Personalities

If you're a Fire element, your study room should honor your natural passion, visibility, transformation while balancing your tendency toward burnout, aggression.

Best Layout

Warm task light pointed at work surface. No blue-white light.

Colors That Support You

Primary: Red, orange, purple. Fire energy thrives in summer conditions — lean into those tones. Shape: Triangular forms.

Essential Decor

Warm desk lamp, amber glass, inspiring art

What to Avoid

Most fire people overload their study room with their own element's energy. You need balance from Water + Earth to feel truly at home.
