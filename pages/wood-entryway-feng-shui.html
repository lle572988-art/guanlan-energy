🌲 Wood Element Entryway — Feng Shui Guide for Wood Personalities

If you're a Wood element, your entryway should honor your natural growth, expansion, upward momentum while balancing your tendency toward restlessness, overexpansion.

Best Layout

Tall plant by entrance. Vertical storage.

Colors That Support You

Primary: Green, teal. Wood energy thrives in spring conditions — lean into those tones. Shape: Rectangular forms.

Essential Decor

Tall branches in vase, wooden shoe bench, plant

What to Avoid

Most wood people overload their entryway with their own element's energy. You need balance from Metal + Earth to feel truly at home.
