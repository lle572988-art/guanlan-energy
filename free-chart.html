What Each Palace Means

Your chart above displays twelve palaces arranged in a fixed grid. The Life Palace (命宮) reveals your core character and life direction. The Wealth Palace (財帛宮) shows how money flows to and from you. The Career Palace (官祿宮) governs vocation, reputation, and professional timing. Other palaces cover relationships, health, travel, property, parents, siblings, children, friends, and inner fortune.

Each palace contains major stars (such as Zi Wei, Wu Qu, Tai Yin) and auxiliary stars that modify their expression. Stars can also carry Four Transformations (Si Hua 四化) — Lu (prosperity), Quan (power), Ke (recognition), and Ji (obstruction) — which activate different themes during specific life periods.

Understanding Your Birth Hour

In Zi Wei Dou Shu, your birth hour is measured in two-hour branches, not clock hours alone. The twelve branches — Zi (11pm–1am), Chou (1–3am), Yin (3–5am), and so on — determine where the Life Palace sits and how all other palaces rotate around the board. If you were born near a branch boundary, adding your birth location helps align true solar time for maximum accuracy.

Not sure which branch applies? Each branch covers a two-hour window in local time. When in doubt, use the midpoint of the window you remember — or book a professional reading to verify palace placement with solar time correction.
