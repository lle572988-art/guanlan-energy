Career Acceleration Through Feng Shui: The 7-Day Office Energy Makeover

Day 1: Clear the Base

Empty the space under your desk completely. Clean the floor and walls behind your monitor. Energy can't rise through clutter. Day 1 is purely subtractive — don't add anything yet.

Day 2: Command Position Audit

Assess your seating position relative to the door. If you're back to door, implement the mirror hack. If facing a wall, add a landscape image. This single adjustment creates more career energy shift than any object you could buy.

Day 3: Dragon and Tiger Rebalancing

Move all tall items to your left side. Keep the right side low and clear. Add a small plant on the left front corner — your Dragon activation point. If your left side is lower than your right, career advancement will feel like pushing against a current.

Day 4: Color Energy Infusion

Add one navy blue item (authority), one green element (growth), and one small red accent (visibility). Even a navy mouse pad, a green plant, and a red pen holder are enough to shift the energetic baseline.

Day 5: Light Strategy

Replace cool overhead bulbs with warm alternatives. Add a directional desk lamp at eye level in front of you. Light from above diminishes presence. Light from in front of you enhances it.

Day 6: Sound and Scent Layer

Add sandalwood (career stability) or rosemary (mental clarity) as a scent. Add a small desktop water fountain or nature sounds during focused work. These create the lasting impression of 'this person has their space together.'

Day 7: Settle and Observe

Don't change anything. Sit in your adjusted workspace for a full workday and notice: do conversations go differently? Is there less friction? The first week creates the most visible change.
