The 14 Main Stars of Purple Star Astrology

The celestial council that shapes your destiny

Zi Wei 紫微 — The Emperor Star

The central star of the entire system. Zi Wei represents authority, nobility, and command. In a strong palace, it indicates natural leadership and a dignified presence. In a weak or empty palace, it may suggest a person born with great potential who needs to actively develop their authority. Full guide: Zi Wei star meaning →

Tian Ji 天机 — The Strategist

Star of wisdom, analysis, and strategy. Tian Ji governs the mind — intellect, planning, and the ability to navigate complexity. Associated with the Wood element. Full guide: Tian Ji star meaning →

Tai Yang 太阳 — The Sun

Represents reputation, visibility, generosity, and public life. A bright Tai Yang indicates warmth, influence, and recognition. When dimmed or afflicted, it can indicate over-giving or reputational struggles. Full guide: Tai Yang star meaning →

Wu Qu 武曲 — The Wealth Star

Metal element star governing finance, discipline, and military-style determination. Wu Qu rules tangible wealth, career fortitude, and executive decision-making. Full guide: Wu Qu star meaning →

Tian Tong 天同 — The Harmony Star

Water element star representing ease, emotional flow, and natural talent. Tian Tong favors creative, artistic, and diplomatic life paths. Full guide: Tian Tong star meaning →

Lian Zhen 廉贞 — The Integrity Star

A complex star combining passion, discipline, and moral conviction. Lian Zhen can manifest as political acumen, artistic intensity, or legal/regulatory expertise. Full guide: Lian Zhen star meaning →

Tian Fu 天府 — The Treasury Star

Earth element star governing stability, accumulation, and conservative strength. A counterpart to Zi Wei in the southern branch of the system. Governs long-term wealth preservation. Full guide: Tian Fu star meaning →

Tai Yin 太阴 — The Moon

Star of beauty, emotion, real estate, and feminine energy. Tai Yin governs intuition, financial flow through property, and aesthetic sensibility. Full guide: Tai Yin star meaning →

Tan Lang 贪狼 — The Talent Star

Wood-Water star of versatility, social talent, artistic skill, and desire. Tan Lang can excel in entertainment, social networking, or creative industries. Full guide: Tan Lang star meaning →

Ju Men 巨门 — The Gate of Darkness

Water star governing communication, eloquence, and also dispute. Ju Men can indicate a powerful voice (public speaking, law, media) or challenges with interpersonal conflict, depending on placement. Full guide: Ju Men star meaning →

Tian Xiang 天相 — The Assistant Star

Water star of service, support, and diplomacy. Tian Xiang individuals are natural mediators, helpers, and those who thrive in supporting roles with integrity. Full guide: Tian Xiang star meaning →

Tian Liang 天梁 — The Elder Star

Earth star of wisdom, healing, longevity, and protective energy. Tian Liang is associated with medicine, teaching, and the role of the wise elder. Full guide: Tian Liang star meaning →

Qi Sha 七杀 — The Seven Killings

Metal star of bold action, risk, and breakthrough. Qi Sha drives revolution, entrepreneurship, and decisive change. It can indicate high achievement or high volatility. Full guide: Qi Sha star meaning →

Po Jun 破军 — The Destroyer

Water star of demolition and reconstruction. Po Jun breaks old structures to build new ones. Associated with bold career shifts, military service, or any path requiring radical change. Full guide: Po Jun star meaning →
