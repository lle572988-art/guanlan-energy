The 14 Main Stars of Purple Star Astrology

The celestial council that shapes your destiny

Zi Wei 紫微 — The Emperor Star

The central star of the entire system. Zi Wei represents authority, nobility, and command. In a strong palace, it indicates natural leadership and a dignified presence. In a weak or empty palace, it may suggest a person born with great potential who needs to actively develop their authority.

Tian Ji 天机 — The Strategist

Star of wisdom, analysis, and strategy. Tian Ji governs the mind — intellect, planning, and the ability to navigate complexity. Associated with the Wood element.

Tai Yang 太阳 — The Sun

Represents reputation, visibility, generosity, and public life. A bright Tai Yang indicates warmth, influence, and recognition. When dimmed or afflicted, it can indicate over-giving or reputational struggles.

Wu Qu 武曲 — The Wealth Star

Metal element star governing finance, discipline, and military-style determination. Wu Qu rules tangible wealth, career fortitude, and executive decision-making.

Tian Tong 天同 — The Harmony Star

Water element star representing ease, emotional flow, and natural talent. Tian Tong favors creative, artistic, and diplomatic life paths.

Lian Zhen 廉贞 — The Integrity Star

A complex star combining passion, discipline, and moral conviction. Lian Zhen can manifest as political acumen, artistic intensity, or legal/regulatory expertise.

Tian Fu 天府 — The Treasury Star

Earth element star governing stability, accumulation, and conservative strength. A counterpart to Zi Wei in the southern branch of the system. Governs long-term wealth preservation.

Tai Yin 太阴 — The Moon

Star of beauty, emotion, real estate, and feminine energy. Tai Yin governs intuition, financial flow through property, and aesthetic sensibility.

Tan Lang 贪狼 — The Talent Star

Wood-Water star of versatility, social talent, artistic skill, and desire. Tan Lang can excel in entertainment, social networking, or creative industries.

Ju Men 巨门 — The Gate of Darkness

Water star governing communication, eloquence, and also dispute. Ju Men can indicate a powerful voice (public speaking, law, media) or challenges with interpersonal conflict, depending on placement.

Tian Xiang 天相 — The Assistant Star

Water star of service, support, and diplomacy. Tian Xiang individuals are natural mediators, helpers, and those who thrive in supporting roles with integrity.

Tian Liang 天梁 — The Elder Star

Earth star of wisdom, healing, longevity, and protective energy. Tian Liang is associated with medicine, teaching, and the role of the wise elder.

Qi Sha 七杀 — The Seven Killings

Fire star of bold action, risk, and breakthrough. Qi Sha drives revolution, entrepreneurship, and decisive change. It can indicate high achievement or high volatility.

Po Jun 破军 — The Destroyer

Water star of demolition and reconstruction. Po Jun breaks old structures to build new ones. Associated with bold career shifts, military service, or any path requiring radical change.
