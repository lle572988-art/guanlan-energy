Zi Wei Dou Shu vs BaZi

Which Chinese astrology system tells you more? · 紫微斗數 vs 八字

Two systems dominate Chinese natal analysis: BaZi (八字, Four Pillars / Eight Characters) and Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數, Purple Star Astrology). Both use birth date and time, but they answer questions through entirely different architectures.

BaZi: The Elemental Formula

BaZi compresses your birth moment into four pillars — year, month, day, and hour — each with a heavenly stem and earthly branch. The resulting eight characters reveal your dominant and deficient elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), ten-year luck pillars, and broad life themes. BaZi excels at elemental diagnosis: what energy you need more of, which industries harmonize your chart, and when major luck shifts occur.

Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Life Map

Zi Wei constructs a twelve-palace board with star placements unique to your chart. Where BaZi tells you that Metal is strong and Water is weak, Zi Wei tells you that Wu Qu in your Career Palace with a Power Transformation means promotion through disciplined expertise in your forties. The granularity is palace-specific and star-specific.

Which Is More Accurate?

Neither is universally "more accurate" — they measure different dimensions. BaZi is superior for elemental balance and macro timing. Zi Wei is superior for domain-specific questions: What does my spouse palace reveal? When does my wealth palace activate? What career path aligns with my star configuration?

Professional practitioners typically use both. For beginners exploring Chinese astrology in English, Zi Wei is often more intuitive because the visual twelve-palace board maps directly to life areas you already think about.

Quick Comparison

  • Structure: BaZi = 4 pillars / 8 characters · Zi Wei = 12 palaces / 100+ stars
  • Best for: BaZi = elements, luck cycles · Zi Wei = life domains, star timing
  • Visual: BaZi = text formula · Zi Wei = chart board
  • English resources: Both improving; Zi Wei remains less saturated in English SEO
