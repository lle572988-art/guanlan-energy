## How to Find Your Command Position

1. **Map your room.** Draw your floor plan. Mark doors, windows, and traffic paths.
2. **Identify the "safe" wall.** This is the wall that gives you the longest diagonal sightline to the door.
3. **Anchor your desk there.** Place your primary screen so that your back is to the solid wall, and you face the room entrance.
4. **Test for 3 days.** Measure your deep work hours before and after. Most people see a 30-40 minute gain by day two.
