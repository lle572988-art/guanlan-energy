2. The Metal-Water Placement Guide

Metal governs structure, systems, and precision. Place metallic objects (a brushed aluminum monitor stand, copper desk lamp) in the Northwest (乾) sector of your workspace. Water governs cash flow and adaptability. Position a small fountain, a dark surface, or a fluid screen-saver visual in the North (坎) sector. Together, they form the 金生水 generative cycle—metal nourishes water, water amplifies financial liquidity.

Avoid placing fire elements (red lighting, electronic heat sources) between these two zones. The fire element melts metal and evaporates water, creating energetic friction that translates to stalled deals and operational bottlenecks.
