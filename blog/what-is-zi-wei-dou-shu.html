What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?

A complete beginner's guide to Purple Star Astrology · 紫微斗數

Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數), known in English as Purple Star Astrology, is a Chinese natal chart system that maps over one hundred stars across twelve life domains called palaces. It was refined during the Song Dynasty and reserved for imperial use before spreading through master-student lineages over centuries.

How Zi Wei Dou Shu Works

Your chart is calculated from your birth date converted to the lunar calendar, your Chinese birth hour (a two-hour earthly branch), and optionally your birth location for solar time correction. The algorithm places the Life Palace (命宮), distributes the fourteen major stars, assigns auxiliary stars, and calculates Four Transformations (Si Hua 四化) that mark prosperity, power, recognition, and obstruction themes.

Unlike Western sun-sign astrology, no two Zi Wei charts are identical unless birth data matches exactly. The result is a square board — visually similar to a astrological house wheel — where each cell represents a palace governing a specific life area.

The Twelve Palaces

Each palace answers questions about one domain: self-identity (Life), siblings, spouse, children, wealth, health, travel, social network, career, property, inner fortune, and parents. Stars within each palace describe how that domain expresses in your life — whether smoothly, with struggle, or in cycles of rise and fall.

The Fourteen Main Stars

At the heart of the system are fourteen major stars including Zi Wei (Purple Emperor), Tian Ji (Heavenly Secret), Tai Yang (Sun), Wu Qu (Military Finance), and Tian Tong (Heavenly Unity). Each carries distinct personality and life themes that change expression depending on which palace they occupy.

Why Learn Zi Wei Dou Shu Today?

For English speakers, quality Zi Wei content has historically been scarce — most material is either untranslated Chinese or machine-translated summaries. A properly calculated chart offers actionable insight into career timing, wealth patterns, relationship dynamics, and annual fortune cycles that generic horoscopes cannot match.
