Missing corner feng shui for love

L-shaped flats and clipped corners are common — especially in cities. Classical bagua links Southwest to partnership and West to children and joy. When those sectors are "missing," relationship energy can feel thin — but cures exist.

Identify what is actually missing

Overlay a bagua grid on your floor plan (front door as mouth of chi). A missing SW corner often means the relationship gua is literally not in the built footprint — outdoor patio, neighbor wall, or cut-out shape.

Soft cures without construction

Bright light and earth tones in the SW sector of the whole home (even if the corner is outside). Pairs of objects (two candles, two stones) as symbolism — not clutter. Healthy plants with round leaves in SW when light allows.

Personal Yan Nian direction

Ba Zhai maps Yan Nian (延年) — your relationship bearing. Face that direction during meals and important conversations; sit there when you want closeness. Personal direction often outperforms generic "put pink in SW" tips.
