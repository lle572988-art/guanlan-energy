Kua 6 — The Heaven: Your Personal Feng Shui Directions

乾 · Qian · The Heaven. A natural strategist — clear-eyed, principled, built to lead from the front.

If your personal Kua is 6, you belong to the West Group (东四命 / 西四命). Your four auspicious directions — and four to soften — are fixed. Someone with Kua 1 gets a completely different map. This page is only for Kua 6.

How to use your Kua 6 directions

For Kua 6, treat directions as action lines — not superstition, but where your body points when you sleep, work, and enter the home.

  • Bed: Headboard on the Northeast wall (NE) — Tian Yi (天醫) supports recovery and deep sleep.
  • Desk: Face West (W) when working — Sheng Qi (生氣) is your strongest wealth-and-action line.
  • Main door: If you can choose, an entrance opening toward West or Northwest (NW) welcomes supportive energy. Avoid aligning the door with East (E).
  • Conversations: Face Southwest (SW) for relationship talks — Yan Nian (延年) softens friction.
