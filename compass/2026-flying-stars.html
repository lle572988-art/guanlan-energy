2026 Flying Star Feng Shui Forecast — Year of the Fire Horse

2026 is 丙午 (Bing Wu) — the Year of the Fire Horse — and the third year of Period 9 (九運, 2024–2043). In classical Xuan Kong flying star feng shui, nine “stars” shift across the eight directions plus center every year. They are not planets; they are energy labels that describe what tends to gather in each sector of your home — wealth momentum, illness, celebration, conflict, or stillness.

This year’s annual chart places Two Black (二黑) in the center — illness and worry energy at the heart of every floor plan. Five Yellow (五黄) flies to the Northeast, the sector that demands the most respect this year. The East glows with Nine Purple (九紫), the reigning star of Period 9, while the Southwest holds Eight White (八白), still a potent wealth star.

These positions are the same for every building on Earth in 2026. What changes is your floor plan: a northeast-facing home greets guests with Five Yellow at the door; an east-facing house parks Nine Purple at the entrance. That is why generic “declutter the southeast” advice fails — you need your layout overlaid on the annual grid.

The three stars that matter most in 2026

Five Yellow (五黄) — Northeast · the sector to respect

Five Yellow is earth energy turned heavy — the star associated with accidents, sudden illness, and “nothing goes right” weeks. In 2026 it occupies the Northeast. If your living room, master suite, or front door sits on the northeast side of your plan, this is your priority zone.

Do: keep the area clean, quiet, and well-lit; add metal (brass bowl, six Chinese coins, white/grey decor); use the room lightly if possible.
Don’t: drill walls, renovate, leave sharp metal clutter, or host loud gatherings here. If the northeast is unavoidable (your only bedroom), metal cures and stillness matter more than panic.

Nine Purple (九紫) — East · the star of the decade

Nine Purple is fire — joy, visibility, weddings, promotions, and the kind of luck that gets photographed. During Period 9, this star carries extra weight. In 2026 it lands in the East, making east-side rooms natural activation zones.

Refresh paint, add warm lighting, celebrate milestones here, and keep the space social. If your kitchen or dining room sits east, family gatherings amplify the star. Pair fire with balance — a candle plus good ventilation, not an overheated clutter pile.

Eight White (八白) — Southwest · the wealth workhorse

Eight White is earth — stable prosperity, savings, and momentum from consistent effort. It was the dominant wealth star of Period 8; in Period 9 it still pays rent, especially in the Southwest this year.

Treat southwest sectors as income zones: tidy desk, active business planning, crystal or ceramic earth accents. Avoid water features that drown earth unless a practitioner tailors element balance to your specific chart. This is where “work on wealth” literally means sit here and do the work.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 2026 flying star chart?

Two Black sits in the center. Stars then fly to Northwest (3), West (4), Northeast (5), South (6), North (7), Southwest (8), East (9), and Southeast (1). This grid applies to every home; your facing direction determines which rooms fall in each sector.

Where is Five Yellow in 2026?

Northeast. Check whether your bedroom, kitchen, or main door occupies the northeast side of your floor plan. If yes, prioritize metal cures and avoid renovation there this year.

Do stars change if my house faces another direction?

Flying stars vs Ba Zhai Kua — which do I need?

When do 2026 stars take effect?

Most schools switch annual stars around Chinese New Year or Li Chun (early February). Monthly stars change every calendar month — track them on the heatmap before moving furniture or starting construction.
